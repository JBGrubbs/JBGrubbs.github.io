This project examines the role of moral incongruence in compulsive sexual behavior. Moral incongruence is the experience of engaging in a behavior one believes is wrong. The work asks how moral beliefs about sex influence self-reported distress, perceived addiction, and help-seeking. It examines patterns of moral conflict, the effects of pornography use, and how these factors bear on clinical assessment and public health recommendations. Collaborations with the International Sex Survey consortium have tested these ideas across dozens of countries, religions, and cultures.
