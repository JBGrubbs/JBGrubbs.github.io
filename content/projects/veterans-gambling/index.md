The **Gambling Outcomes in Veterans (GOVET)** project expands the systematic study of gambling disorder by drawing on five decades of treatment data from the Gambling Treatment Program at the Louis Stokes Cleveland VA Medical Center, led by Heather Chapman, PhD. Its aims are to:

1. Compile roughly 50 years of Gambling Treatment Program data into an accessible, analyzable database.
2. Establish a clinical profile of veterans with severe gambling problems.
3. Identify risk factors for gambling problems among veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces.
4. Compare veterans in inpatient treatment with gamblers and veterans in the broader community.
5. Identify individual-difference and treatment variables associated with recovery.

The **Gambling in Women Veterans Initiative** treats gender as a key variable in veterans' gambling. Women veterans are often missing from gambling research, and this initiative examines how impulsivity, substance use, suicide-attempt history, and co-occurring compulsive sexual behavior differ for them. The initiative is supported by 50x4Vets funding.
