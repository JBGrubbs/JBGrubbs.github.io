Funded by the International Center for Responsible Gaming (ICRG), this Center of Excellence (November 2026 to October 2029) studies how sports betting, alcohol, and other substance use interact. It brings together four studies:

1. **Meta-analysis** of the literature linking sports betting with alcohol and substance use.
2. **In-depth interviews** with sports bettors who vary in betting style and drinking.
3. **Daily ecological momentary assessment (EMA)** combined with linked records of actual bets, to see how drinking and betting relate from day to day.
4. **A pilot micro-randomized trial** testing a brief, text-message mindfulness intervention delivered at moments of risk.

The Center builds on the BAAAD Lab's longitudinal work showing that sports bettors drink more, and binge drink more often, than other adults.
