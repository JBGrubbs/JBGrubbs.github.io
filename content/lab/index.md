        The BAAAD Lab is based in the Department of Psychology and the Center on Alcohol, Substance use, And Addictions (CASAA) at the University of New Mexico. We study gambling and sports betting, compulsive sexual behavior, and the alcohol and substance use that often comes with them. Our work combines large longitudinal panels, daily (EMA) methods, clinical treatment samples, and qualitative interviews, and we aim to make our methods, materials, and data as open as possible.
