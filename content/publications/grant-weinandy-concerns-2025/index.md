abstract: 'Comorbidity rates between Compulsive Sexual Behavior (CSB) Disorder and addictive disorders range from 14% to 44%. However, it remains unclear whether individuals who report concern about CSB are more likely to engage in, and experience problems with, other addictive behaviors. This study aimed to examine whether those who report concern about CSB engage in and experience problems with other addictive behaviors more than those who do not report concern about CSB. Adults (N = 4,072) from a census-matched U.S. sample reported their frequency of engagement with several substances/behaviors. Those who reported past-year engagement also completed measures assessing the number of problems they experienced. Correlations and independent-sample t-tests showed higher engagement and problems with all substances/behaviors in those who reported concern about their CSB compared to those who did not report concern about CSB. This suggests distress across multiple addictive areas in those who are concerned about their CSB, regardless of diagnosis, highlighting a need for prevention and screening across addictive domains in this population.'
